This is a forward-looking mapping for the current 15-row diagnostic assessment,
not a rescore, release authorization, or change to a frozen or historical
artifact. The diagnostic assessment is 59/100 and the historical 46-row
commercial assessment remains 52/100
([remaining workflow](remaining-workflow.md#latest-local-evidence--2026-10-01),

machine-produced evidence. It excludes human semantic judgment, authorized
rights decisions, buyer/product-owner decisions, and an independent replay
attestation. A ceiling is an inferred planning bound, not a score claim.
